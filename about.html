Our story

Young people, making an older idea new again.

BrightYouth is an independent, youth-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2024. We create meaningful community programs and joyful learning experiences for children in K–8.

C

Care is a practice.

We make it visible in the way we welcome people, listen closely, and keep showing up.

P

Play is serious work.

Games, ideas, and shared creativity make learning feel less like a requirement and more like an invitation.

B

Belonging changes things.

Every program is designed to help young people feel known, valued, and connected.
